Section 02

How the Strategy Brain Works: Renko Bricks, Wilder ATR & Supertrend

🧒 Think of Renko Like Building with Lego Blocks
On a normal 1-minute chart, a candle spawns every 60 seconds even if Gold barely moved — creating lots of zig-zag confusion. A Renko Chart ignores the clock and ONLY adds a new brick when price actually travels a fixed step size (Brick Size).
• Going Up (Continuation): Price rises +1 Brick Size → prints 1 new Green Brick.
• Turning Down (Reversal): To flip direction from Green to Red, price must drop 2× Brick Sizes (filtering out tiny wobbles!).
📐 Exact Quantitative Formulas (No Black Boxes)
• Daily Wilder ATR(5): Uses completed prior daily candles only (zero lookahead bias) with Wilder RMA smoothing.
• Dynamic Brick Size: brick_size = round_to_tick(Daily ATR(5) / ATR_Divisor). Gold uses Divisor 15 (~$6–$7 bricks); Scalp/FX/Indices adapt via Category Profiles.
• Supertrend (Len 1, Factor 2.1): Computed natively on completed Renko brick True Ranges (ATR = brick range), producing an institutional staircase Stop/Reverse regime line.
Section 03

Model E Dual-Order Architecture & 5-Minute Confirmation Gate

1️⃣ 5-Minute Confirm Clock

When 2 consecutive Renko bricks align with Supertrend, the bot does NOT rush in blindly. It starts a 5-minute countdown (pending_confirm). If momentum collapses during those 5 minutes, the trade is cancelled cleanly (saving a loss!).

2️⃣ Leg 1: Usual (3B-TP) + Leg 2: Runner (+1B)

Once 5 minutes hold, Leg 1 (Usual) enters at market with a fixed +3 Bricks Take Profit and 3-Brick Safety Stop. Simultaneously, the bot places a Capital.com Native STOP Working Order (+1B) — Leg 2 only fills if price breaks even higher!

3️⃣ Automatic Breakeven (BE) Lock

The moment Leg 1 hits its +3B Take Profit and banks cash, the live watcher automatically moves Leg 2's Stop-Loss to its entry price. From that second forward, your Runner rides massive multi-hour trends with zero downside risk.
